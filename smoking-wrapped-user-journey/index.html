Smoking Wrapped user journey

The app shows the user's smoking pattern back to them, then points them toward local support.

The journey

01

Profile

Capture simple age, gender, and agreed context fields.

02

Split

Ask whether their smoking is mostly social or more regular.

03

Questions

Three route-specific inputs calculate their yearly pattern.

04

Wrapped

Show cigarettes, packs, time, and a possible cost stat.

05

Support

Link the result to a reason to stop and a clear next step.

Two routes

1Social smoker

  • 1Cigarettes on a typical night out
  • 2Nights out per month
  • 3Main trigger or moment

2Light smoker

  • 1Cigarettes on a day they smoke
  • 2Days per week they smoke
  • 3Main trigger or routine

Output

Core stat 274

Total cigarettes smoked in a year.

Core stat 14

Packs smoked across the year.

Core stat 23h

Time spent smoking.

Potential stat £210

Estimated cost, if useful for the final message.

From result to action

The final screen can connect the user's result to what they care about most: skin, fitness, money, or feeling in control. On venue iPads, a QR code gives them a private way to continue on their own phone.
