About

Designed to
make sense.

I'm Shea Wilson, a second-year Digital Design student at Ulster University. I design interfaces and I build them.

My biggest project right now is Brain Dump, a full-stack app where you type or speak freely and AI sorts everything into tasks, routines, ideas and projects. I designed it in Figma, built it with Next.js and Supabase, and it has real users in beta. It started as a personal project because I kept losing track of things across too many apps.

I've been using AI tools since ChatGPT first came out and more seriously for about a year. Claude Code to build, Perplexity and NotebookLM for research. I test most things when they come out. It's changed what I can get done on my own.

Most designers don't build. I think that's a mistake.
