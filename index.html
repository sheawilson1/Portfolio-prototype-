About

I build the tools
I wish I had.

  • Design engineer
  • Studying Digital Design

I start in Figma and Paper, then build with Claude Code and Codex, so clients can click through something real while their feedback can still change it.

At Influential I design and build products for clients and internal tools for the team, like a PowerPoint add-in that puts the brand’s assets inside PowerPoint. Launch isn’t the end of it. When I suspected people were dropping out of Smoking Snapshot before the last questions, I added save points earlier so their answers still counted.

I build things for myself too. I built Brain Dump because I kept losing track of things across too many apps. I’ve also made my own dictation app, and a wallpaper that swaps in a different museum painting every morning. Winslow Homer’s The Herring Net is the one I keep coming back to.
