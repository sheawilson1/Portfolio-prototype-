About

It isn’t designed
until it’s live.

I start in tools like Figma and Paper, then move into code early. Clients get something real to click through while the design is still changing, so their feedback shapes the product before it goes live.

A lot of my work takes numbers people skim past and makes them personal. Smoking Snapshot turns “I only smoke socially” into a count for the year. JumpStart turns a month of ad data into a few plain sentences a client can act on.

01

Real before it’s finished

Clients try a working version on their own devices early on, while there’s still room to change the design around what they tell me.

02

Design and code, one person

Nothing gets lost in a handoff. The person who drew the details is the one who builds them.

03

Launch is the start

I don’t ship something and leave it. On Smoking Snapshot I suspected people were dropping out before the last questions, so I added save points earlier in the journey and kept their answers even when they left partway.
