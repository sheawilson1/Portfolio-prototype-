About

Most designers don’t build. I think that’s a mistake.

Design engineer · Digital Design, Ulster University

I’m Shea. I design in Figma and Paper, then get into code early, so clients can click through something real while their feedback can still change it. I’ve used AI tools since ChatGPT first came out, and now I build with Claude Code and Codex.

At the moment I’m designing and building public health campaign tools and a reporting product for agency clients. Launch isn’t the end of it. On Smoking Snapshot I suspected people were dropping out before the last questions, so I added save points earlier in the journey and kept their answers even when they left partway.

Brain Dump, my own app, started because I kept losing track of things across too many apps.
