About

It isn’t designed
until it’s live.

I start in Figma and move into code early, so the thing people approve is the thing that ships. I build with AI tools like Claude Code and Codex, and spend the time they save on the details that make something feel finished.

A lot of my work is about making numbers land: a year of smoking turned into one figure, a heart-attack statistic turned into a heartbeat, a month of ad data turned into a few sentences a client can act on.

01

Prototype in the real thing

I test ideas in the browser, on real devices and with real data, so problems show up early.

02

Design and code, one person

Nothing gets lost in a handoff. The person who drew the details is the one who builds them.

03

Ship it, then measure it

I add analytics before launch. On Snapshot, that’s how we knew 53% of people went on to answer the follow-up questions.
